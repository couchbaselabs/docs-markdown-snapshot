# Ask AI

> You can ask our AI chatbot questions about Couchbase Capella to get personalised responses that are cross-referenced with our documentation. 

Ask AI is available from every page in Capella and keeps track of your conversation as you navigate to different pages. This feature is available as a public preview.

## [](#managing-conversations)Managing Conversations

Ask AI takes information from the current conversation into account when answering a new question.

To start a new conversation, click **New chat** () on the chat panel.

To view your chat history, click **Chat history** () on the chat panel. You can reopen previous conversations by selecting them from the list.

You can Delete conversations by clicking **Delete Chat** () beside each conversation in the chat history.

## [](#responsible-use)Responsible Use

Ask AI uses Large Language Models (LLMs) from industry-leading AI providers. The specific model can change based on the complexity of your queries or your specific task.

While using Ask AI, make sure that you:

* Do not enter sensitive data in your conversations.
* Verify any suggestions made by Ask AI for accuracy before taking action.

To use Ask AI, you must first accept [Capella’s Supplemental Terms](https://www.couchbase.com/capella-feature-specific-terms/).

## [](#best-practice)Best Practice

To get the most out of Ask AI, remember the following rules:

* Ask concise, direct questions and state your goal.
* Begin a new conversation for each new task.
* Be clear in your language and use Couchbase terminology when possible.

## [](#turning-ask-ai-off)Turning Ask AI Off

You can opt out of AI features from the **Settings** tab by unchecking the **Enable Capella iQ** option.
