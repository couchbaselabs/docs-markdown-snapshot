# Alert Reference

> Use this reference for the kinds of alerts that Capella AI Services can send you, including their alert conditions and descriptions. 

## [](#metric-based-alerts)Metric-Based Alerts

If there is a measurable change in your Capella AI Services resource use, Capella sends notifications that include information about potential causes and remedial actions to investigate.

Capella delivers notifications for these alerts to you by:

* Displaying a [message banner](../../cloud/clusters/monitoring/alerts.md#view) in the Capella UI.
* Sending email to users who [enable email notifications](../../cloud/clusters/monitoring/alerts.md#cloud-get-alerts-through-email) for their accounts.
* Delivering messages to a third-party notification system through an [alert integration](../../cloud/clusters/monitoring/alert-integration.md) if configured for the project.
